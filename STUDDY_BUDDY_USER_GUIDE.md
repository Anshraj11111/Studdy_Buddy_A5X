# 🎓 Studdy Buddy - Complete User Guide Script
### "Ek Platform Jahan Students Milke Seekhte Hain"
---

## 🎤 INTRO SCRIPT (Presenter ke liye)

> "Namaste! Aaj main aapko Studdy Buddy ke baare mein bataunga - ye ek aisa platform hai jahan tum sirf padhai nahi karte, balki milke seekhte ho, ek doosre ki help karte ho aur grow karte ho!"

---

## 📱 STEP 1 - SIGNUP / LOGIN

### Agar pehli baar aa rahe ho:
1. **studdybuddy.a5x.in** pe jao
2. **"Join Studdy Buddy"** button click karo
3. Apna naam, email, password bharo
4. **"I am a Student"** select karo
5. Agar school ne tumhe **School Code** diya hai toh **School Password** field mein dalke free access lo!
6. Submit karo - tum directly Dashboard pe aa jaoge ✅

### Agar pehle se account hai:
- Email + Password se login karo
- Ya **"Sign in with Google"** se seedha login karo

> **💡 Tip:** School Code wale students ko premium courses **FREE** milte hain!

---

## 🏠 STEP 2 - DASHBOARD

Dashboard tumhara **home base** hai. Yahan dikhega:

- **Tumhari Recent Activity** - tumne kya kiya recently
- **Pending Doubts** - jinke answers aana baaki hain
- **Communities** - popular posts
- **Quick Links** - sabhi features fast access ke liye

> "Ye aapka personal control center hai - ek nazar mein sab kuch!"

---

## 📚 STEP 3 - RESOURCES (Courses)

**Navbar mein "Resources" click karo**

### Yahan 2 tarah ke courses hain:

#### 🆓 Free Courses
- Seedha **"Enroll for Free"** click karo
- Turant access mil jaata hai

#### 💎 Premium Courses
- **School Code wale students:** "Enroll for Free (School Access)" - instant access!
- **Regular students:** "Purchase Course" → QR code scan karo → UPI se pay karo → Admin verify karega → Access milega

### Course ke andar:
1. **Modules** dikhengi list mein
2. Kisi bhi module pe click karo
3. **Video lectures** play honge
4. Progress automatically track hota hai

> "Ye bilkul YouTube jaisa hai - but sirf tumhare liye, tumhare school ke hisaab se!"

---

## ❓ STEP 4 - MY DOUBTS

**Navbar mein "My Doubts" click karo**

### Doubt Poochna:
1. **"+ Post a Doubt"** button click karo
2. **Title** likho - ek line mein question
3. **Description** mein detail se samjhao
4. **Topic** select karo (Robotics, AI/ML, etc.)
5. Image ya video attach kar sakte ho
6. **Post** kar do!

### Kya hota hai baad mein:
- Dusre students aur mentors tumhara doubt dekhenge
- Jo best match hoga wo tumse **automatically connect** ho jaayega
- Tum **Chat** mein directly baat kar sakte ho
- Doubt solve hone pe **"Mark as Solved"** karo

> "Koi bhi sawaal chota ya bada nahi hota - poochho, seekho, aage badho!"

---

## 👨‍🏫 STEP 5 - MENTORS

**Navbar mein "Mentors" click karo**

- **Expert mentors** ki list dikhegi - real industry professionals
- Filter karo by skill (Robotics, Programming, AI, etc.)
- Mentor ka card dekho - unki expertise aur experience
- **"Message"** button click karo - seedha chat shuru!
- **"Video"** button click karo - live video call karo!

> "Ghar baithe expert se seekho - bilkul free!"

---

## 💬 STEP 6 - CHATS

**Navbar mein "Chats" click karo**

- Tumhari saari **conversations** yahan hain
- Mentor ke saath, doston ke saath - sab ek jagah
- **Online (green dot)** = abhi available hai
- **Video call** button bhi hai har chat mein
- **Search** karo kisi specific person ko

> "WhatsApp jaisa but sirf padhai ke liye!"

---

## 🏫 STEP 7 - MY SCHOOL

**Navbar mein "My School" click karo**

- Tumhare **school ka channel** yahan hai
- School ke baaki students se baat karo
- School ki **important announcements** yahan aati hain
- Teachers aur seniors se seedha connect karo

> "Poora school ek jagah - no drama, sirf padhai!"

---

## 🌐 STEP 8 - COMMUNITIES

**Navbar mein "Communities" click karo**

### Feed (Posts):
- Apne **projects share karo** - photos, videos ke saath
- Doston ki posts **Like** karo ❤️
- **Comment** karo, discussion karo
- Post karte waqt **category** select karo

### Connections (LinkedIn jaisa):
- **"Discover"** tab mein naye log dhundho
- **"Connect"** bhejo - jaise Facebook ka friend request
- **"Pending"** mein incoming requests dekho
- **"Connected"** tab mein apne sab connections dekho
- Connected ho jaane ke baad **"Message"** button aata hai - seedha chat karo!

> "Yahan gali dene ki permission nahi hai - sirf positive vibes! 😄"

---

## 🏆 STEP 9 - REWARDS

**Navbar mein "Rewards" click karo**

### XP (Experience Points) kaise milte hain:
| Activity | XP |
|---|---|
| Doubt post karna | +10 XP |
| Doubt solve karna | +20 XP |
| Community post karna | +5 XP |
| Like milna | +2 XP |
| Daily login streak | +5 XP/day |

### Leaderboard:
- Top students ka ranking dekho
- Kya tum #1 ban sakte ho? 🥇

> "Padhai karo, points kamao, legends bano!"

---

## ⚙️ STEP 10 - PROFILE & SETTINGS

### Profile Update karna:
1. Top-right corner mein apne naam pe click karo
2. **Profile** ya **Settings** pe jao
3. Photo upload karo
4. Bio, skills, education add karo
5. Social links (GitHub, Instagram) add karo
6. **Save** karo

> "Acha profile = zyada connections = zyada opportunities!"

---

## 🔔 NOTIFICATIONS

- **Bell icon** (🔔) top navbar mein
- New connections, likes, comments ki notifications
- Real-time updates - refresh ki zaroorat nahi!

---

## 🌙 DARK MODE

- **Moon icon** (🌙) navbar mein
- Dark/Light mode toggle karo
- Aankhon ko sukoon milega raat ko!

---

## 📱 MOBILE PE USE KARNA

- **studdybuddy.a5x.in** mobile browser mein open karo
- **"Add to Home Screen"** option aayega - bilkul app jaisi feel!
- Sab features mobile pe bhi kaam karte hain

---

## ❗ IMPORTANT RULES

1. **Gali ya abusive language** - bilkul nahi chalegi, post block ho jaayegi
2. **Dusron ki respect** karo
3. **Real information** bharo - fake accounts nahi
4. **Study related** content share karo
5. Personal contact info (phone number) share mat karo

---

## 🆘 PROBLEM AA RAHI HAI?

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Login nahi ho raha | Password reset karo - "Forgot Password?" |
| School channel nahi dikh raha | Admin se school channel create karwao |
| Course access nahi mil raha | School password check karo ya payment karo |
| Message nahi ho raha | Pehle connect karo phir message |
| Page load nahi ho raha | Refresh karo (Ctrl+R) |
